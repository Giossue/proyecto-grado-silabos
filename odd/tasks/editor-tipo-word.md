# Template editor more like Word

Goal: the admin template visual editor (apps/web/src/components/domain/configuration/TemplateVisualBuilder.tsx and its tools) is reorganized like Word: a ribbon with tabs and labelled buttons, a side panel for field and table options, and the essentials on top. How the template is stored and the syllabi do not change. Branch: main (direct commits allowed). Runner: vitest (`apps/web`, `apps/api`).

## Specs

- S1 — A ribbon with tabs, like Word, with labelled, grouped buttons. Agreed with the user (2026-11-26):
  - **Inicio**: Deshacer, negrita, cursiva, subrayado, color, alineación, listas.
  - **Insertar**: Tabla, Dato automático (the variables, today `{ }`), Respuesta del docente, Contenido predefinido.
  - **Diseño**: Papel, orientación, márgenes, tipo y tamaños de letra, colores de tablas, títulos. This also covers the styles that today are JSON-only.
  - **Campo**: shown only when a field is selected: quién lo llena, límite de caracteres, «mostrar solo si…».
- S2 — Advanced options go in a separate panel: clicking a field or a table opens a panel on the right with its options, like Word's «Formato» panel. The sheet stays clean and options only show when needed.
- S3 — On top, only the essentials: Guardar, Vista previa and Ver como docente. Technical things like «Editar JSON» are hidden in a «Más» menu.
- S4 — Nothing changes in how the template is saved or in the syllabi; only the screen changes.
- S5 — The bugs found in the browser audit get fixed along the way:
  - «Editar JSON» opens the saved template, not the current draft. Its «Aplicar y reemplazar» saves immediately and throws away the builder draft with no warning. It must start from what is being edited, or warn clearly.
  - Console errors: an insert point `<div>` inside a `<p>` in an empty new section (around TemplateVisualBuilder.tsx:1136), `flushSync was called from inside a lifecycle method` when inserting a repeatable table, and `Blocked aria-hidden … descendant retained focus`.
  - The sticky toolbar covers the top of the sheet.
- S6 — User feedback after trying T1–T3, with a screenshot of the panel (2026-11-26): «hay como un redundancia de indicaciones me están diciendo y es muy tosco el movimiento por ejemplo cuando aparece o desaparece cosas por dar click en ciertas partes, porque incluso por eso mismo como que se mueve la hoja, y ojo, no debería salir como eso de tablas o lo que sea si es que el campo no lo es y es lento, será porque estámos en dev?». The screenshot shows the panel «Opciones del campo» / «Campo sin nombre», with a «Quién lo llena» label over an «Editable ▾» dropdown whose menu repeats «¿Se puede editar?» (No editable / Editable) and also holds «Ponerle nombre». Below it: «Tablas repetibles — Este campo no tiene tablas repetibles — + Nueva tabla repetible». Requirements:
  - (a) remove the redundant labels: one clear control for who fills the field, and the name edited directly, not hidden in that menu;
  - (b) no jumps: opening or closing the panel, the contextual tab, or anything that appears on click must not move the sheet; transitions must be smooth;
  - (c) only show the options that apply to the field: no «Tablas repetibles» section when the field has none;
  - (d) check whether the slowness is caused by dev mode or by the editor, and fix the editor's share.
- S7 — User (2026-11-26), with a screenshot of the T4 work in progress, where the Campo tab of the ribbon and the floating right panel show the same options: «mira... mejor ponlo fijo arriba no lo pongas flotando como ahora». Field options live only in the ribbon fixed at the top (the Campo/Sección contextual tab). The floating right panel is removed (this replaces S2). The ribbon is a full-width band docked at the top, not a floating card. Selecting a field opens the Campo tab automatically.
- S8 — User (2026-11-26), with a screenshot of the editing header bar (← Volver a la vista … Ver como docente, theme toggle): «borra esto y ahi pon mejor la cinta de opciones el titulo igual borralo». While editing, that header bar and the page title («Plantilla de sílabo / El formato del sílabo: secciones y campos») are removed. The ribbon takes the header's place at the very top. «Volver a la vista», «Ver como docente» and the theme toggle move into the ribbon (e.g. back arrow at the left of the tabs; Ver como docente next to Vista previa; the theme toggle inside «Más»).
